About BlockWorld

A small game project built around creativity, exploration and voxel-style gameplay.

🎮 The Game

BlockWorld is an original voxel game where you can explore a block-based environment, mine materials, place blocks and build your own creations.

🌐 Play Online

The game runs directly in your browser, making it easy to play from a computer without downloading a traditional desktop launcher.
